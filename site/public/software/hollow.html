33 GB of WHAT?

Nobody goes looking for that

One app on my Mac had left 33 GB of crash reports in a folder I had never opened. About 180,000 of them.

You need something that puts it in front of you.

When the number drops, see why

A ring and the free-space number sit in your menu bar. No Dock icon.

Click it to see what changed on your disk: today, over 7 days, or over 30 days.

Nothing moves until you say so

Cleanup suggestions collect into a basket, and you go through them on a review sheet. Items go to the Trash and you can put them back.

Hollow never empties the Trash. System folders are refused.

It keeps up and it doesn’t hang

Hollow reads the disk once, then follows changes. A folder that hangs gets skipped and retried, so the app doesn’t freeze waiting for it.

1% CPU at idle with the map window open, measured on my Mac.

1.4 s for a cleanup scan of a disk with 1.95 million folders.

0 network connections. It stays on your Mac, and you can read the source.

empty the Trash when you’re ready. 22 GB free!
