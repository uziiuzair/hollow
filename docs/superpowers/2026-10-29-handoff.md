## What the founder still has to do by hand

These checks can't be done by an agent on this Mac, either because they need a pointer, a GUI or a permission prompt, or because the binding safety rules forbid them.

1. **Status item.** Confirm by eye that the ring and the free-space number show in the menu bar (macOS 26 groups status items, and the menu bar here is crowded).
2. **Map navigation with a real pointer and keyboard.**
   - Click selects. Double-click opens a folder, on both the map and the side list.
   - The back chevron works, and so do ⌘[, ⌘↑, ⌘↓, Esc, the mouse back button and a three-finger swipe.
   - Right-click and control-click menus show: Open, Show in Finder, Copy Path, Add to Basket, Move to Trash….
3. **Move to Trash on the internal disk.** Agents only ran it against throwaway disk images, and the path checks were verified read-only. Try it once on a folder you don't need, then empty the Trash yourself.
4. **Eject from Finder** while a drive is scanning. The app should let it eject within about 2 s.
5. **Grant Full Disk Access**, then confirm that the folders shown as unreadable fill in without a restart.
6. **Release-build feel.** Check memory and CPU at idle (agents measured 1.16% idle CPU in an optimized build) and confirm the window opens in about 100 ms. Also check the first open after a reboot (cold cache), which agents couldn't reproduce.
7. **Cleanup suggestions ("lenses") in a release build.** Open "Review N GB to clear" and check the suggestions make sense for your disk.
8. **Settings window.** Check menu bar display, launch at login (only from /Applications), warning thresholds, drive tints and excluded folders.
9. **Locked-screen first launch.** Mount a new external drive with the screen locked, then unlock. Updates should carry on (one thread per protected location at most).
